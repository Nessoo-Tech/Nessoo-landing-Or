Nessoo FAQs

  • What is a verified rental exchange?

    A rental marketplace where renters complete identity and income verification once and can be matched to homes they can qualify for, instead of repeatedly submitting the same information to separate listings.

  • What does Nessoo verify?

    Two things: your identity, and the income and assets you report. Landlords and brokers get a confirmed identity and verified income and assets, not an unfiltered personal file.

  • What is the difference between Nessoo and a listing site?

    Listing sites get paid while you search. Nessoo gets paid to end it. You verify once, Homey matches your profile to homes you can qualify for, and landlords with a match come to you.

  • Is Homey making rental decisions for me?

    No. Homey organizes, surfaces, explains and coordinates. It tells you why a match makes sense. You choose.

  • What is preview-before-verification?

    You can preview before you verify, then move forward when the opportunity is worth it: understand the opportunity, know what is being asked of you, and decide what to do next.

  • Does verification guarantee an apartment?

    No. Verification is not approval. A verified profile doesn’t guarantee a lease, an apartment or a particular decision, and it doesn’t replace legally required screening or a property’s own process.
