How it works

The rental process, reversed.

You verify once. Homey matches. Landlords come to you. You choose.

01 / 04

Verify once.

Build a profile that is more than an application. Nessoo confirms who you are and verifies your income and assets, so you never repeat the same work for every apartment.

02 / 04

Set your fit.

Tell Homey what you want and need: budget, timing, location, commute and space. Homey pairs them with your verified income and assets to understand what you can qualify for.

03 / 04

Review matches.

Homey matches your verified profile to homes you can qualify for and explains each match. Landlords with a match reach out. You decide which conversations deserve your attention.

04 / 04

You choose.

Homey tells you which offer to take and why it’s the best one. No black-box decisions. Preview before verification, then move forward when the opportunity is worth it.
