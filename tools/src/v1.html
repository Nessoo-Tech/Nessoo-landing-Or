What is a verified rental exchange?

A rental marketplace where renters complete identity and income verification once and can be matched to homes they can qualify for, instead of repeatedly submitting the same information to separate listings.

What does Nessoo verify?

Your identity, and your income and assets. Rental history and references live in the same profile, which only you control.

What is the difference between Nessoo and a listing site?

Listing sites get paid while you search. Nessoo gets paid to end it. Instead of you searching and applying, Homey matches your verified profile to homes you can qualify for, and landlords with a match reach out to you.

Is Homey making rental decisions for me?

No. Homey organizes, surfaces, explains and coordinates. You choose.

What is preview-before-verification?

You can preview how Nessoo works before you verify, then move forward when an opportunity is worth it.

Does verification guarantee an apartment?

No. Verification is not approval, and it does not replace legally required screening. It gives landlords a confirmed identity and verified income and assets, so the exchange is clearer for both sides.
