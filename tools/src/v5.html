How it works

Verifying is the only move you make.

Everything after that comes to you.

  1. Build a profile that is more than an application. Nessoo confirms who you are and verifies your income and assets, so you never repeat the same work for every apartment.

  2. Tell Homey what you want and need: budget, timing, location, commute and space. Homey pairs them with your verified income and assets to understand what you can qualify for.

  3. Homey matches your verified profile to homes you can qualify for and explains each match. Landlords with a match reach out to you.

  4. You decide which conversations, viewings and offers deserve your attention. Preview first, then move forward when the opportunity is worth it.

Frequently asked questions

What is a verified rental exchange?+

A rental marketplace where renters complete identity and income verification once and can be matched to homes they can qualify for, instead of repeatedly submitting the same information to separate listings.

What does Nessoo verify?+

Two things: your identity, and your income and assets. That verified profile is what Homey uses to match you, and what a landlord with a match sees.

How is Nessoo different from a listing site?+

A listing site makes you search, apply and pay at every door. On Nessoo you verify once, Homey matches your profile against units on the market, and landlords with a match reach out to you.

Is Homey making rental decisions for me?+

No. Homey organizes, surfaces, explains and coordinates. It tells you why each match makes sense. You choose.

What is preview-before-verification?+

You can see how the exchange works and what is being asked of you before you verify, then move forward when the opportunity is worth it.

Does verification guarantee an apartment?+

No. Verification is not approval. A verified profile doesn’t guarantee a lease, and it doesn’t replace legally required screening or a property’s own process.
