The brothers who built it

We got beat by the old way. So we ended it.

Last summer, Aiden did everything right. He applied, paid the fees, sent the paperwork, and watched the apartment he wanted go to someone who got there an hour earlier. Weeks later, the same thing happened to Gabe. Same city, same broken process.

So we built Nessoo: verify once, get matched on fit, and never start from zero again.
